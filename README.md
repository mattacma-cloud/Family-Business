This introduction is about the Long Game series of mod projects and my thinking regarding the various game stages of BattleTech and how they manifest themselves in BEXT. If you have read the introduction on one of the other mods, you can skip down to the details on this mod in the next section.
BattleTech is not really optimised for a long, multi-decade career that feels rewarding, as it’s better to start new game in ten years for the early and mid-game thrills, rather than just slogging on as an elite, well equipped colossus. With the development of the Lore Packs underway, this has really come to the fore in my thinking, and I am looking to how the interesting middle game can be extended, and how more consequence can be added to the late game. 
The current shared experience of BattleTech and BEXT largely and very generically runs to:
1.	Surviving the ***Early Game*** can be quite the test, as resource management, low-skull missions, and keeping your little ‘Mechs alive through each mission consumes much of your time.  ***Early Game*** in BEXT is a real test of your skills, especially on higher difficulty levels, where you fight various foes while struggling against tight finances. As I view it, ***Early Game*** is a combination of High Risk/Low Reward situations. By low reward, I mean that in the context of the whole game. A single new ‘Mech or weapon can be a massive gain early, but against later gains, is not really all that great.
2.	***Mid Game*** transitions you to a more stable company and the beginning of your real ‘Mech and pilot specialisation. The ***Mid Game*** gets pilots into heavier chassis, along with new pilot skills, and larger enemy reinforcement groups that test your newfound power. For many starting later in the timeline, this can also coincide with the Clan Invasion. The advancing timeline also brings new technology, introducing Helm Memory Core technologies and as already mentioned, the Clans. ComStar missions add another layer to ***Mid Game***. ***Mid Game*** is also where you can really mix risk v reward, playing safe or rolling the dice when you have material and cash reserves or are in a desperate state.
3.	***Late Game*** is the power game. It often revolves around Heavy and Assault ‘Mechs, though a mixed force with good technology is often the better bet. Enemies will drop massive alpha strikes but are not as agile as the player. This can send you seeking the Clans for a challenge or hunting down rare Star League-era relics. However, the generally lower risk, high reward ***Late Game*** setting eventually yields ever diminishing returns for player satisfaction, and that’s when it’s time to start a new career,

So, with all that in mind and a development map reaching forward decades for the Lore Packs, I got to thinking about what would encourage someone to play right through with the one company, and from that came the ideas for the following mods, which I’ll be developing alongside the Lore Packs:
•	Company Decorations [https://github.com/mattacma-cloud/Decorations]
•	No Easy Fix [https://github.com/mattacma-cloud/No-Easy-Fix]
•	Family Business – In development
•	Rusted Metal – In development
•	Hard to Resolve – In development

My hope is to create a series of mods that extend the mid-game in a way that is not overwhelming, but keeps a player deeply engaged with their crew, having to keep a close eye on finances as materials, the Argo, and crew events effect cash flow, and always with an eye on what equipment they need as they move forward. By making that which was common and easily obtained in the late game harder to come by, the mid-game can extend for longer, and for the masochistic amongst us, extend the early game significantly as well.

**Family Business**
Family Business will be the largest of the Long Game mods, and builds on what one of the BattleTech game designers recognised as a missed chance with the game. Family Business will add family, crew politics, love, loss, children and aging pilots to the game. Choices made can have decades long effects as you and your family grow, age and suffer joy and loss. Some results are quite dark, and the interwoven set of 28 core story lines mean that who is who in the crew, what they do, what they pilot and all the events they have experienced have real weight. This also means that if you kick off in 3025, by 3045, your roster of ageing pilots will be losing their skills as the next generation come to the fore. Who do you keep, who stays and how does that affect everyone else, become critical questions with short- and long-term risk and reward considerations.
This also ties in the almost 800 events across vanilla, BEX, Timeline, FeelGood, and the lore pack mods. Family Business will read from their results to convert their outcomes into the scores that Family Business tracks. Events you used to fly through now have real consequence. Disgruntled pilots will leave you, and other events will bring new pilots and crew to you, so how you behave has significant short- and long-term effects on your team.
This mod will be the largest of the Long Game mods, tying what occurs across the others together in a story form that hopefully gives a lot more depth and emotional investment into the crew you have built.
This also means that risk v reward, in terms of people, becomes very important, as you grow and then replace your crew over time, consider who your heir will be and possibly have those you offended come back to try and kill you. The impact on Risk / Reward will likely only be moderate, but the emotional triggers should hopefully tie players to their company for longer.
